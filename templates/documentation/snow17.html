Snow-17 (experimental)

This endpoint runs the Snow-17 snow accumulation and ablation model (Anderson, 1973 & 2006) at a point, forced by the daily CMIP6 downscaled data. Each model/scenario combination that has precipitation, maximum temperature, and minimum temperature is run separately, giving daily snow water equivalent (SWE) and outflow (melt plus rain leaving the snowpack) for 1965–2100.

This is an experimental, uncalibrated model run. The default parameters are from the American River Basin (Shamir & Georgakakos, 2007), not Alaska, and the model is driven by daily mean air temperature (the mean of tasmax and tasmin). Results should be treated as illustrative.

The site elevation (meters) can be set with ?elevation=<meters>. Otherwise, the 1km mean elevation from the ASTER GDEM is used.
Any of the model parameters listed below can be overridden, e.g., ?scf=1.2&mfmax=1.5.

Notes

  • Every run starts with no snow on January 1, 1965, so the first winter of the historical run is a spin-up period. Future scenario runs (2015–2100) start from the model state at the end of the same model's historical run.
  • The 7ModelAvg run is Snow-17 forced by the averaged forcing data, which is not the same as the average of the individual model runs.
  • CESM2 and the EC-Earth3-Veg ssp126 scenario are not available, since they lack temperature data.
  • Water year summaries only include complete water years, so they cover 1966–2014 for the historical scenario and 2016–2100 for future scenarios.

Design snow load

The design summary estimates the 50-year ground snow load (2% annual exceedance probability) from the annual peak SWE of each water year. Historical runs are summarized for water years 1966–2014, and future scenarios for 2040–2069 and 2070–2099.

  • A generalized extreme value (GEV) distribution is fit to each era's annual peaks by the method of L-moments (Hosking, 1990). Years with no snow are treated as a mixed distribution: the GEV is fit to the snowy years only, and the 50-year probability is adjusted for the fraction of snow-free years. If at least 98% of years are snow-free, the 50-year load is 0. If fewer than 10 years in an era have snow, no fit is made and the values are null.
  • The load is the weight of the snow water equivalent: 1 mm of SWE is 1 kg/m2, or about 0.0098 kPa (0.205 lb/ft2).
  • This is a ground snow load from an uncalibrated model driven by climate model data. It is intended for comparing eras and scenarios, not for structural design. Building codes such as ASCE 7 apply further factors (exposure, thermal, slope, importance) and drift and rain-on-snow loads to get a roof load.

Output Description
swe Snow water equivalent of the snowpack (mm)
outflow Water leaving the snowpack, or rain on bare ground (mm/day)
peak_swe Maximum SWE in the water year (mm)
peak_swe_date Date of the maximum SWE, or null if there was no snow
snow_days Number of days in the water year with SWE of at least 1 mm
total_outflow Total outflow in the water year (mm)
n_years, n_snow_free_years Number of water years in the era, and how many of them had no snow
mean_peak_swe_mm Mean annual peak SWE in the era (mm)
swe_mm, load_kpa, load_psf 50-year annual peak SWE (mm) and the equivalent ground snow load (kPa and lb/ft2)
gev Fitted GEV location, scale, and shape (Hosking's sign convention: shape > 0 has a bounded upper tail)
